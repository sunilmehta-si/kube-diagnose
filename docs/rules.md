# Rules

Every finding names one of these rules. IDs are stable: a rule is retired, never renumbered. `kube-diagnose rules KD101` prints one. This file is generated from `internal/diagnose/catalog.go` (`make docs`).

The tens digit says where the problem is: x00-x29 pulling the image and running the container, x30-x39 its configuration and volumes, x40-x49 scheduling, x50-x59 storage, x60-x69 Services and networking, x70-x79 creating pods, x80-x89 workloads and x90-x99 nodes. KD2xx continues an area once its KD1xx numbers are used up, and two areas ran past their ten there: KD270-KD274 are Services and networking, and KD290-KD292 are workloads. KD3xx continues an area whose KD2xx numbers are used up: KD300 and on are running the container.
